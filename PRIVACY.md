## 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)

歡迎使用 **DailyBless 每日祝福**（以下簡稱「本 App」或「我們」）。我們極為重視中高齡長輩及所有使用者的個人隱私與數位安全。本隱私權政策旨在清楚向您說明本 App 如何處理相關資訊。

### 1. 免帳號與零個資收集 (No Account Required)
* 本 App 無需註冊或登入任何帳號即可直接使用。
* 我們**不會**收集、索取、儲存或傳輸任何個人識別資訊（包括但不限於：真實姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼、住址、身分證字號等）。

### 2. 相簿存取權限說明 (Photo Library Permission)
* 當您點擊「儲存圖片」按鈕時，本 App 會請求「將圖片儲存至相簿」的權限（`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`）。
* 此權限**僅用於**將精美問候卡片寫入儲存至您的裝置相簿中。
* 我們**絕不會讀取、掃描、查看或上傳**您相簿中的任何既有照片或影片。

### 3. 本地端資料儲存 (Local Data Storage)
* 為了提供良好的操作體驗，您的偏好設定（包括：已收藏卡片、文化區域切換設定、VIP 會員解鎖狀態）僅儲存在您的**個人裝置本地端**（透過 iOS 系統內建的 `UserDefaults` 機制）。
* 這些本地資料完全屬於您個人裝置，不會上傳至任何雲端伺服器。

### 4. 廣告與第三方服務 (Advertising & Third-Party Services)
* 本 App 免費版本整合了 **Google AdMob** 行動廣告服務，以維持營運並提供每日免費好運卡。
* **不追蹤使用者 (No Tracking)**：本 App 嚴格遵守隱私保護規範，**絕不進行跨 App 或跨網站的使用者追蹤**。我們未啟用 Apple 應用程式追蹤透明度（ATT），亦不收集、不提供您的廣告識別碼（IDFA）給廣告廠商。
* 所有廣告均以**非個人化廣告 (Non-Personalized Ads)** 模式載入。
* Google AdMob 可能會收集技術性的裝置診斷資訊與效能指標，以確保廣告正常顯示與防範無效流量，相關處理均遵循 [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

### 5. App 內購買項目 (In-App Purchases)
* 本 App 提供一次性買斷之「VIP 終身尊榮方案」以永久免除廣告。
* 所有付款與訂單流程均由 Apple 官方之 App Store 安全付款機制（StoreKit）直接處理。我們不會取得、亦無法儲存您的信用卡資訊或付款憑證。

## English

Welcome to **DailyBless** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of seniors, families, and all our users. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

### 1. No Account Required & No Personal Data Collection
* DailyBless is completely accessible without any user account, registration, or login.
* We do **NOT** collect, request, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as real name, email address, phone number, address, or government ID).

### 2. Photo Library Access
* When you tap the "Save Image" button, the App requests permission to add photos to your photo library (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`).
* This permission is used **solely** to save your selected greeting cards directly to your device's photo album.
* We **NEVER** read, access, scan, or upload your existing photos or videos.

### 3. Local Data Storage
* To preserve your user experience, your preferences (including favorite cards, selected regional feeds, and VIP lifetime unlock status) are stored **strictly locally on your device** using iOS `UserDefaults`.
* These local settings remain on your device and are never sent to external servers.

### 4. Advertising & Third-Party Services
* The free tier of DailyBless integrates **Google AdMob** to display occasional sponsored ads.
* **Strict Non-Tracking Policy**: DailyBless does **NOT** track users across third-party apps or websites. We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization, and we do not collect or share your advertising identifier (IDFA) with advertisers.
* All advertisements are served strictly in **Non-Personalized mode**.
* Google AdMob may collect technical device diagnostics and performance metrics to prevent fraud and deliver ads in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 5. In-App Purchases
* DailyBless offers a non-consumable "VIP Lifetime Pass" to remove all ads permanently.
* All purchase transactions are processed securely and directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect, view, or store your credit card or financial billing information.

### 6. Changes & Contact
* We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page.
* If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please contact us:
  * **Email**: allen80321@gmail.com
  * **Repository**: https://github.com/LeePoH/DailyBless
